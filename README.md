# Harder Nether

A minecraft mod by Rasa Novum

Currently does the following:

### Nether

- Applies onFire effect in the nether after a short period of time, requiring the player to bring a source of fire resistance.
    - Early game fire resistance comes from foods that are assigned short durations of fire resistance upon consuming.
- Increases hunger burn in the Nether.

### Deep Dark

- Adds a unique timer to each player that tracks the duration they have been in the biome for.
- Displays messages to the player based on status of timer (ie. just entered, <25% of time remaining, status cleansed).
- Corruption debuff added upon timer reaching given duration in the Deep Dark, damages the player constantly until cleansed.
- Totem of Light item added, used to cleanse the player and any other players around them in an area of effect.
